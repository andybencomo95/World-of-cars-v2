About World of Cars

Customize your car, explore 5 unique tracks and race online with virtual coins — free, in 8 languages, no download.

Frequently asked questions

Is World of Cars free to play?

Yes. World of Cars is free to play in your browser with no download. Customize your car, race 5 unique tracks and join online lobbies using virtual coins. No real money is involved.

How do I play World of Cars?

Press PLAY OFFLINE to customize your car and start a 2-lap race, or PLAY ONLINE to join a lobby. Drive with WASD or arrow keys, SPACE for turbo boost, H for horn and R for headlights.

How does online mode work?

Create or join a lobby with a 4-letter code. The minimum bet is 1 virtual coin and the race winner takes 90 percent of the pot. Quitters forfeit their bet.

Which languages does the game support?

Eight: English, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, Portuguese and Russian. Tap the globe button in the header to switch instantly.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs entirely in a modern web browser on desktop and mobile. Offline progress is saved in your browser.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Touch controls appear automatically on touch devices, with steering, brake, boost and horn buttons.
